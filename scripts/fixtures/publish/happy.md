## Body

Hot days are hard on dogs, and "just a quick walk" can turn into heatstroke faster than most owners expect.

## Spot the early signs

Heavy panting, thick drool and a bright red tongue are early warnings.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">[1]</a></sup>
